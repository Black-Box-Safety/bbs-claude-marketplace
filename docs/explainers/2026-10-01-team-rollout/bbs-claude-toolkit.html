What Claude is at BBS

An assistant at your desk. It follows the BBS playbooks, looks through live windows into the systems you already use, and hands you a draft.

Playbook (a skill)
The BBS way of doing one job, written down. Claude picks the right one when your request matches. You don't have to name it.
Binder (a plugin)
A set of playbooks for one area, like back office or the website. Yours are already on your computer.
Window (a connector)
A live view into a system you already use. Parker switches these on for each person. Business Central and Grainger are read-only.
Shortcut (a command)
A word you type that starts with a slash, like /bbs-what-can-i-do.
You
You check it, you decide, you send.

Skill, plugin and connector are the words you'll see in Claude. Type /bbs-explain-claude for the plain-language version.

What it will never do

In Claude Code, the app your setup installs, these rules are switched on for everyone. They are why it is safe to try things there.

  1. You ask
  2. Claude looks
  3. Claude drafts
  4. You check and send

In Claude Code, nothing sends without your Allow.

In Claude Code, Claude can send an email or Teams message only after you click Allow on that send, or put a draft in your Outlook Drafts for you to send. The ordinary Claude chat window does not have this rule, so use Claude Code for this work.

Reads Business Central, never changes it.

Business Central and Grainger are read-only for Claude. It cannot post, edit or delete a record.

The right signer on the right line.

If you're in Sales, Claude signs your packets once Parker has your signature on file. It signs each line as the right person, and never for a customer. Anything it can't sign comes back flagged. Outside Sales, it doesn't sign for you.

Your mailbox is yours.

Claude reads your mail only when you connect it and ask. It never reads anyone else's. /bbs-my-voice learns from your own Sent Items and stops if the mailbox isn't yours.

Rules built in.

No invented numbers: if Claude doesn't know, it flags it. Certification wording follows the channel: DVBE for California state work, SDVOSB for federal.

The full one-page policy is the AUP you sign with Parker.
