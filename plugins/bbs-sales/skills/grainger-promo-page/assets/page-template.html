03 / How the order runs

Nothing about your workflow changes

The vehicle is already competed. The catalog is the one your unit already buys from. The only thing that changes is the supplier on the line item.

Step 01

Send the list

Item numbers and quantities, by email or through your buyer. A quote comes back on the contract price, with current stock status and ship dates for those items.

Step 02

Issue the purchase order

The order rides the California MRO Statewide LPA, 7-25-51-02.03. No new solicitation, no sole-source justification, no duplicated evaluation.

Step 03

Receive and report

Black Box Safety takes the order and delivers, backed by Grainger's national distribution network. The spend reports as diversity participation.

04 / Why route it this way

Same catalog. Same contract. Now it counts.

Your unit already buys these items. Bought direct, the spend is simply spend. Routed through Black Box Safety, the same items at the same contract price become reportable supplier diversity participation for your department, with no price premium to absorb and no change to how the order is placed.

Black Box Safety is a California-certified Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise. We take the order, own the account, and deliver.
