# OBS 4007(C) Packet C — assembly and field reference

> **Scope:** field IDs, pages and how this packet is assembled. Who signs which line, what
> the State fills, the DVBE box on an LPA order and the signature limit are owned by
> `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/ca-form-rules.md`; this file points there.

CDCR sends Packet C for a Fair and Reasonable / Exempt price request — in practice a NASPO
LPA order. It **arrives partly filled**: the solicitation number, dates, buyer, ship-to
and often the line items are the agency's, and the attachments may be bound into one PDF
or sent as separate files (Parker, 2026-10-01). So this profile is an *assembly*, not one
fixed page map:

1. `scripts/fill_packet_c.py` takes the packet plus any `attachments` listed in the
   inputs, and identifies every page by its own header with the form table in
   `ca-form-rules.md`. It prints which Packet C forms are present, where, and which are
   missing.
2. It fills each identified form from the field IDs in its `FORM_FIELDS` map. A field the
   agency's copy has renamed is a `[MISSING — NEEDS INPUT]` line, never a guess.
3. Filling and signing go through `naspo-quote`'s `fill_ca_forms.py`, the one vetted
   engine: the signer rules and limit, each signature's page checked against its form,
   field IDs validated, and **every value the agency entered kept** (reported as
   `kept agency value`).
4. The filled documents are merged into one PDF in the order given.

### OBS 4007(C) front page, Rev. 03/2025

BBS fills: `OBS RFQ 4007(C) - Business Name`, `- Business Contact Person`,
`- Business ContactTitle`, `- Business Contact Phone`, `- Business Contact Email`,
`- Business Address L1` / `L2`, `- Federal Employer Identification Number FEIN`,
`-  Sellers Permit` (two spaces after the dash), `- SB/MB/DVBE Cert`, `- Cert Exp Date`,
the `- SB Checkbox` / `- MB Checkbox` / `- DVBE Checkbox` boxes (on-state `On`), and
`OBS RFQ 4007 (C) Manufacturer -Yes` / `-No` (on-state `Yes`).

Irregular IDs worth knowing: the signature and its date are `OBS RFQ 4007C)` — the
opening parenthesis is missing; the release date is `Rlease Date`; the manufacturer boxes
have a space in `4007 (C)`; address line 5 is `OBS RFQ 4007(C) - Address 5`.

## Where this packet carries the State's own fields

| Page | The State's fields |
|---|---|
| 1 | Solicitation Number, Release Date, Due Date (`OBS RFQ 4007(C) - Solicitation`, `- Rlease Date`, `- Due Date`) |
| 2 | The whole "Additional solicitation details" section: delivery days, method of award, freight terms, buyer name/email/phone/fax, shipping address |
| 3 | The buyer's fax number for fax bids |
| 8 | "Other" attachment text |

The fill never writes these; whatever the agency entered is kept.
