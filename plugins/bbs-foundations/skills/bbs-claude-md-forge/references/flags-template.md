# FLAGS.md template (per desk or per buyer folder)

Blameless by design: an entry means the review process WORKED. Log liberally.

| Date | Skill/step | Bucket | What happened | Caught before send? | Happened more than once? | Fixed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-15 | naspo-quote | factual error | CalRecycle 74 pulled last quarter's solicitation # | yes | no | (Parker: triage) |

Pick one bucket:

| Bucket | Use when |
|---|---|
| factual error | A number, date, cert, or other system-of-record fact was wrong |
| buyer preference | The buyer wanted it a different way, and that preference was not written down |
| missing information | The draft went ahead without a fact it needed |
| style | Wording, tone, or layout. The facts were fine |

One line per event. "Caught before send: yes" means a near-miss (good catch). "no" means an incident — tell Parker the same day, not at triage.

"Happened more than once?" is yes when an earlier line is the same kind of miss. A repeat is supposed to become a rule in the owning skill and a case in `docs/evals`, in the same change. Wednesday is the hour for that pass. The calendar entry stays the monthly FLAGS triage in `docs/ADMIN-RUNBOOK.md`.

Monthly triage turns the remaining entries into skill fixes. Verified-correct cases graduate into the golden eval set.
