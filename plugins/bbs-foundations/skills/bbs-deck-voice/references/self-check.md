# Slide Self-Check

Run every slide through this before returning copy. These are the failure modes that actually cost credibility or break Parker's voice. If any answer is "no" (or "yes" for the red flags), fix it before delivery.

## Voice

1. Does the headline assert a point rather than label a topic? (If it could be a folder name, rewrite it.)
2. Does the slide lead with what the agency gains, with BBS features demoted to proof?
3. Is there exactly one idea on the slide?
4. Are bullets fragments (roughly three to five words), four or fewer?
5. Do numbers and contract citations carry the persuasion instead of adjectives?

## Red flags (any of these present = fix)

6. **Em dash anywhere.** Replace with period, comma, or colon.
7. **Marketing adjectives**: premier, world-class, cutting-edge, best-in-class, trusted, proven (as filler), comprehensive, seamless, robust. Cut or replace with a fact.
8. **Filler openers**: "we are pleased to," "in today's environment," "as you know," "leverage our expertise."
9. **Restating the obvious** to a professional buyer.

## Federal integrity

10. **DVBE appears in the deck.** Federal decks use SDVOSB only. Remove it.
11. **Overstated federal traction.** Any claim implying a large federal base or incumbency. Reframe to set-aside eligibility, GSA access, and early-mover opportunity.
12. **CA contracts framed as serving federal customers.** Reframe CA record as proof of execution capability being transferred to federal.
13. **GSA Schedule number**, if cited, reads exactly #47QSWA23D000Y.

## Close

14. Is there exactly one specific CTA, not a vague "let's partner"?
