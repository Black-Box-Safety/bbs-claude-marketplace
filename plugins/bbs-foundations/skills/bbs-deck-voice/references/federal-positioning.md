# Federal Positioning Reference

Read this before writing any certification, contract-vehicle, or track-record copy for a federal deck. It exists because federal context differs from BBS's dominant California context in ways that break credibility if mishandled in front of a contracting officer.

## Certification discipline

| Context | Certification | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Federal agencies, GSA, set-asides | **SDVOSB** (VA-verified) | The only certification that appears in a federal deck |
| Sourcewell / NPPGov / cross-state SLED | **SDVOSB** | National cooperative context uses the federal credential |
| California state, CA higher ed | DVBE | **Never appears in a federal deck** |

If source material references DVBE, convert the framing to SDVOSB. Never print both. Never call BBS a generic "small business" without the SDVOSB credential attached.

## Contract vehicles for federal

- **GSA Schedule #47QSWA23D000Y.** Cite exactly. This is the frictionless-purchase path for federal buyers.
- **SDVOSB set-asides.** BBS is eligible for set-aside competitions in safety, PPE, MRO, and body armor. This is the primary federal growth lever.
- **Sourcewell Master Agreement #091924-BBS.** National body armor, 38% off MSRP, tiered volume discounts at $50K / $100K / $250K+. Available to any public agency, usable in SLED-adjacent federal contexts.

## The federal-traction honesty rule

BBS federal volume is currently low and actively growing. This is the single easiest place to lose a room's trust. Rules:

- **Never** imply a large federal install base or federal incumbency.
- **Never** present California contracts as if they serve federal customers.
- **Do** position around three honest strengths: SDVOSB set-aside eligibility, the GSA vehicle, and an early-mover opportunity for the agency to work with a veteran-owned alternative before the relationship is crowded.
- Frame the CA track record as **proof of execution capability**, then transfer it: "The team that became the largest small-business seller to the State of California is now building its federal footprint." True, credentialed, and forward-looking.
