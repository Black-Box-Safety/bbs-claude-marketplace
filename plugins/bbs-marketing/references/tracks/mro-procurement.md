# Track: MRO / Procurement

The contract-vehicle expression. It sells access to already-competed MRO agreements and
DVBE spend credit to California public agencies: CSU and UC campuses, cities, counties,
special districts, and state departments.

Shared identity (wordmark, palette values, company facts, credential list) lives in
`references/core.md`. This file covers only what makes the MRO expression distinct. Every
factual assertion below traces to `references/CLAIMS.md`; build pieces with
`brand-check --scope mro`.

## Visual dress

- Ground the piece in BBS navy `#051C2C`. Gold `#B19800` is the DVBE payoff color and is
  rationed: hero payoff line, DVBE references, the `[BOX]` in the wordmark. Nothing else.
- Grainger red `#C8102E` is the action color: CTA blocks, section eyebrows on light pages,
  infographic accents. Never let gold and red carry equal weight in the same block. One
  leads, the other supports.
- Set everything in Roboto, Grainger's typeface. One family, varied by weight. Headlines
  Roboto 900, body 400, emphasis 500/700, eyebrows 700 uppercase at ~0.1em tracking.
  Embed fonts as woff2 data URIs; never link a webfont URL.
- Green `#2F7D32` appears only on "this stays the same" confirmations. It has no other job.
- Layout default: letter, two sides. Front navy-dominant with a working-tradesperson photo
  hero. Back paper `#F7F6F3` dominant. Hero photos ride in an `<img>`, not a CSS
  background-image.
- Category art is line icons in navy or red stroke. Do not use BBS product photography on
  this track. The on-hand library is tactical and reads to the wrong buyer.

### Grainger marks: what this track may and may not do

Use the official Distributor Alliance lockups in `brand/assets/logos/`. Rules:

- May place the Grainger Distributor Alliance lockup and the alliance seal on the piece,
  typically bottom-right of the hero and in the footer legal strip.
- May use Grainger red as a design color, and Roboto as the typeface.
- May state the approved role wording: `Grainger Authorized Tier 1 Diverse Reseller`.
- May show manufacturer brand logos on a white bar as "brands available on contract."
- May not redraw, recolor, outline, rotate, or crop the Grainger wordmark or seal, and may
  not place either on a red field.
- May not use Grainger's own tagline as if it were ours. Evoke that energy in the imagery;
  do not print their line.
- May not lock the BBS wordmark and the Grainger wordmark into a new combined mark. They sit
  as separate marks with clear space between them.
- May not imply BBS is Grainger, or that Grainger endorses the agency's purchase.
- The public-safety track's marks (DGS, NPPGov, the body-armor vehicle) never appear on an
  MRO piece. Cross-scope claims fail `brand-check`.

## Voice register

Calm, procedural, procurement-literate. Write the way a good contract analyst talks: short
declarative sentences, active voice, concrete nouns, no adjectives doing work that a number
should do. Name the zeros out loud (no added cost, no new bid, no new login, no workflow
change), because risk aversion is the public buyer's default posture.

Contrast with the public-safety track: that expression is mission-forward and speaks to
officer outcomes. This one is paperwork-forward and speaks to audit outcomes. If a line
would sound at home in a recruiting video, it belongs on the other track. If it would
survive being read aloud in a procurement review, it belongs here.

Hard style rules: no em dashes or en dashes. Sentence case in headings. No hype cluster
("seamless," "robust," "unlock," "elevate," "cutting-edge"). No rule-of-three padding. Bold
only the few phrases that carry the scan. End on the action or a fact, never on a closer.

## The wedge

**Same Grainger. Same prices. Now it counts.**

Same catalog, same contract price, same fulfillment. The one change: the order routes
through Black Box Safety, a California-certified DVBE, so the spend becomes reportable
diversity participation at no added cost, on the channel the agency already uses.

Second wedge, for agencies without an existing punchout: skip the RFP cycle. Ride a vehicle
that has already been publicly solicited, evaluated, and awarded. No new solicitation, no
duplicated effort, best-value pricing on day one.

The strongest single-glance layout for both is the two-panel comparison: four green-check
items that stay the same (catalog, pricing, fulfillment, workflow) against one navy panel
holding the single change.

NEEDS CLAIM: the mandated DVBE participation goal figure for California state departments
(the "3%" the wedge turns on). Do not print a percentage until it is registered.

## The partnership rule

**Grainger is a partnership, never a pass-through.** BBS is a `Grainger Authorized Tier 1
Diverse Reseller`, and BBS's supply chain is backed by Grainger's national distribution
network. BBS takes the order, owns the account, and delivers.

Banned phrasings, no exceptions: "Grainger handles it," "Grainger ships and we collect,"
"we just process the paperwork," or any construction where Grainger is the subject of the
verb that serves the customer. Rewrite as BBS delivering, backed by Grainger's network. This
is a compliance rule, not a stylistic preference: pass-through language makes BBS read as a
loophole rather than a supplier, which is the fastest way to lose a procurement officer.

The three-step "how it works" block follows the same rule. Step two is "backed by Grainger's
national distribution network," never "Grainger fulfills."

## Buyers

- **Procurement officer.** Accountable for a defensible award and for hitting the agency's
  diversity participation goal. Moved by: an already-competed vehicle, a contract number
  they can cite in the file, reportable spend, no price premium. Lead with compliance.
- **Facilities manager.** Accountable for uptime and a budget that does not move. Moved by:
  same catalog, same price, same delivery, and a supplier who answers the phone. Lead with
  nothing changes.
- **Requisitioner.** Accountable for getting the order placed today. Moved by: the same
  punchout, the same cart, one different vendor tile. Lead with the click, not the policy.

Every piece has to reach at least two of the three. Compliance is the hero for the officer;
nothing-changes is the hero for the other two.

## Piece types

- **Leave-behind.** Two-sided letter, full system. The default after a meeting or site visit.
- **Punchout switch card.** Half-letter or 4x6. One instruction plus one QR. Shows the vendor
  tile to select. Built for the requisitioner and meant to be taped to a monitor.
- **Vendor-fair flyer.** Single side, navy hero, one red QR block, one ask. Readable from
  three feet away across a table.
- **Capability one-pager.** Single side, paper ground. Vehicle table, category grid, contact
  block. Built for a procurement file, so it prints clean in grayscale.

Every piece carries the same navy footer: BBS wordmark, cooperative marks, contact block,
and a one-line legal strip with the vehicle numbers and credentials.
