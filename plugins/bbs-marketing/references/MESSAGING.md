# Messaging

The canonical argument for Black Box Safety, Inc. `core.md` governs identity, `tracks/` governs dress
and voice, `CLAIMS.md` governs every fact. This file governs the argument, and only this file: where
a line here and a line in an older file disagree, this one wins. Facts inside these lines are quoted
from `CLAIMS.md` in its exact approved wording and are not editable at write time. A fact this file
needs and the registry does not carry is marked `NEEDS CLAIM` and cannot be printed until the row
exists.

## 1. Canonical positioning statements

### MRO / procurement (the only approved version)

> Black Box Safety, Inc. sells California public agencies the same Grainger catalog at the same
> contract price with the same fulfillment, and routes the order through a California-certified DVBE,
> `CA DVBE Cert. 2009473`, so routine MRO spend becomes reportable diversity participation at no
> added cost.

Supersedes, as positioning statements:

- `grainger-csu-knowledge.md`: "reports toward the campus's mandated diversity-procurement goal, at
  $0 added cost." Scoped to CSU, and it states goal attainment where `core.md` allows only mechanism.
- `value-prop-intake.md`: "converts routine MRO into reportable DVBE spend at zero added cost, proven
  by Fresno State." Same narrowing to a punchout, and the peer proof is unregistered.
- `copy-guidelines.md`: "The order routes through a California-certified DVBE, so the spend becomes
  reportable diversity participation." Kept as an approved body line in section 4. Alone it carries
  only the change and drops the sameness, the half that removes the objection.
- `tracks/mro-procurement.md`, the wedge paragraph. Closest to canonical; the statement above is its
  one-sentence form and does not change its meaning.

`Same Grainger. Same prices. Now it counts.` stays canonical as the hero headline, not as the
positioning statement, and never carries a piece alone. The descriptor "California-certified DVBE" is
inherited from `tracks/mro-procurement.md`; the credential prints only as `CA DVBE Cert. 2009473`.

### Public safety (the only approved version)

> Black Box Safety, Inc. supplies California law enforcement with body armor, plates, carriers, duty
> gear, and tactical equipment from named specialist manufacturers, on contract vehicles already
> competed and ready to order.

Supersedes: the body tactical brochure's "government contracting company with extensive sourcing
capabilities" framing (generalist, no named brands, opens on the company instead of the category);
the LE/MRO brochure's "local strategic sourcing partner" line (a bridge header, not a position); and
any construction opening a public-safety piece on DVBE credit, spend mechanics, or goal attainment,
which belongs to the MRO track and makes BBS read as a broker.

## 3. Value proposition by audience

Read the "do not lead with" column first. It is the one that stops a piece pitching audit mechanics
to a person who wants gear that works.

| Audience | Track | Headline | Proof | CTA | Do NOT lead with |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requisitioner | MRO | Same cart. One different vendor tile. | Screenshot or name of the tile to select | Scan the QR, sign in, pick the BBS tile | Policy, DVBE mechanics, contract numbers |
| Procurement officer | MRO | Same Grainger. Same prices. Now it counts. | `C-CSUCO-00001768` · `2018-000207` · `7-25-51-02.03` · `CA DVBE Cert. 2009473` | Forward to the punchout owner, or ask us to set it up | Product breadth, brand logos, delivery speed |
| Facilities manager | MRO | Same catalog, same price, same delivery. | `Grainger Authorized Tier 1 Diverse Reseller` · backed by Grainger's national distribution network | Request a contract pricing account | Diversity goals, audit language, cooperative theory |
| Equipment unit | Public safety | Fit, sizing, and lead times we will state up front. | Spec grid, roster brands, honest lead time | Ask for the line card and current lead times | Benefits copy, spend credit, cooperative vehicles |
| Chief / command staff | Public safety | The buy is already competed. | `1-23-84-07` · `091924-BBS` · `PS25650` · one paragraph, no procurement theory | One call with the reorder contact | Product catalogs, brand walls, procurement mechanics |

Every MRO piece reaches at least two of the first three rows. Compliance is the hero for the
procurement officer; nothing-changes is the hero for everyone else on that track.

## 4. Approved headlines and kickers

MRO / procurement:

- Hero: `Same Grainger. Same prices. Now it counts.`
- Second wedge: `Skip the RFP cycle.`
- The change: `The order routes through a California-certified DVBE, so the spend becomes reportable
  diversity participation.`
- Partnership: `Our supply chain is backed by Grainger's national distribution network.`
- Kicker: `For the ones who keep California's campuses running.` One restriction: it echoes a
  Grainger line, so it never sits in the same block as the Grainger wordmark or seal, where it would
  read as reprinting their tagline. Swap "campuses" for the sector on a non-CSU piece.
- Retired: the QR line in `copy-guidelines.md` that states a product count and calls BBS "CSU's
  preferred MRO channel." It prints an unregistered scale figure and an unregistered preference
  claim. Use instead: `Access the Grainger catalog through the channel your campus already uses.`

Public safety, set condensed and all-caps per the track file. Never "End of Watch" in a headline,
product name, or campaign name, and never urgency copy against a long-lead item.

- Category: `Armor, plates, and carriers, on contract.`
- Specialist: `The brands your officers already ask for, by name.`
- Access: `Already competed. Ready to order.`
- Long lead: `Made to order. Reserves ship in order received.`

scoped `all`. Contract numbers are track-scoped, so add them beneath the boilerplate at the piece's
scope, never inside it. Cross-scope numbers in one block fail `brand-check`.

**25 words.** Black Box Safety, Inc. supplies MRO, safety, and public safety equipment to California
public agencies. CA DVBE Cert. 2009473. SDVOSB. Based in El Cajon, California.

public agencies, on contract vehicles that have already been publicly solicited, evaluated, and
awarded. The company is an SBA-certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business and holds CA
DVBE Cert. 2009473. Black Box Safety operates from El Cajon, California.

public agencies: campuses, cities, counties, special districts, and state departments. Agencies buy
through contract vehicles already publicly solicited, evaluated, and awarded, so there is no new
solicitation to run and no price premium to absorb. Black Box Safety takes the order, owns the account,
and delivers. The company is an SBA-certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business and
holds CA DVBE Cert. 2009473, so agency spend routed through it becomes reportable diversity
participation. Black Box Safety operates from 1950 Cordell Ct., Ste 103, El Cajon, CA 92020.
Contact joey@blackboxsafety.com.
